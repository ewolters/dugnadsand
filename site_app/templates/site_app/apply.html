Applying to the network

An application is a request to be admitted to a chapter. It is read by a person, and admission is a decision that person makes.

An applicant establishes that they are a real and identifiable party. Nothing here asks what an applicant offers, or assesses how well they do it — a system that graded offers would be pricing them, and this one prices nothing.

What each kind establishes

Applying asWhat is checked
A business A trade or business licence and a certificate of insurance, both with the date they run out, and a tax identification number.
A not-for-profit A determination letter and a tax identification number.
An individual No documents. Agreement to the operating policy, and a search of the public registries a chapter checks, run and recorded by a person.
A new chapter A conversation. Nothing on this form substitutes for one.
What submitting this does

It records a request. It admits nobody, and it creates no account. Every credential given below is checked against whoever issued it before any decision is made, and a credential that has run out is treated as absent.

The details submitted here are held to run the review. They are not published, and they are not shared with any admitted organization.
