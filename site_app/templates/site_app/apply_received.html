Application received

Recorded as an application from {{ kind|lower }}. Nothing has been admitted.

Every credential supplied is checked against whoever issued it before a decision is made. A credential that has run out is treated as absent. Where an individual has applied, a search of the public registries the chapter checks is run and recorded by a person.

The decision is made by a person and comes back by email to the address given. There is no account to sign into in the meantime, and no status page: an application is a request, not a queue position.

If something was wrong

A correction can be sent through the contact form. A second application is not needed, and two records for one applicant slow the review down.
