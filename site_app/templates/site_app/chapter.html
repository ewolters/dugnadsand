What this page cannot show

A chapter holds a roster. It has no access to anything inside the organizations it admitted: members, postings, the ledger, projects and material are invisible to it, as they are to every other organization.

This is enforced by the shape of the database rather than by this page choosing not to display it. A chapter carries no relation to any tenant-scoped record in either direction, so there is no query this screen could make even by mistake.
