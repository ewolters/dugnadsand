What a role is, and is not

This page is the roster and the applications addressed to this chapter. Holding a role here grants no view of anybody's records: the role attaches to a login, carries no relation to any tenant-scoped record in either direction, and there is no query this screen could make even by mistake.

Seeing the work of the chapter is a separate thing, and comes from being a member of an organization in it rather than from running it. An officer who is a member sees what every other member sees, and no more.
