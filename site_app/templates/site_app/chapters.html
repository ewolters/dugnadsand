Where there are none

A chapter starts as an application from the people who intend to run it. The named officers are the ones who take that on: admitting organizations in the area, holding the roster, and being reachable by the people in it.

Applying to the network · Statement of operating policy

What a chapter shares

A chapter is what people have in common. Members of every organization in one see each other's offers, needs, projects, work days and material, and may take up any of it — most organizations here are one or two people, and a network where each saw only its own board would be a set of separate boards.

Between chapters there is none of that. The separation is enforced by the database rather than by application code remembering to filter, and it fails closed: a defect makes rows disappear rather than leak.

Holding a role in a chapter is a separate matter from being in one. An officer runs the roster and reads the applications addressed to the chapter; the role by itself grants no view of anybody's records, and carries no relation to them.
