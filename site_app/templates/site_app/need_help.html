Finding help

Contact a group directly · nothing on this page is a form

Dugnadsand takes no requests for help and holds no waiting list. It coordinates supply between the groups that do that work — finding out that a contractor forty miles away has material going to a skip, and getting it to a group that can use it.

The groups below decide what they can do and for whom. Contacting one of them directly is the whole of it: they know what is available this week, what their own rules are, and what happens next.

What this page is not

There is nothing here to fill in, and nothing typed on this site reaches a group. Everything after the introduction happens between a person and that group, by whatever means they list — a phone, an address, a Tuesday morning.

No record of the request exists here. Dugnadsand never learns who asked, what was asked for, or what was decided.
