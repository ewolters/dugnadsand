Finding help

Contact a group directly · nothing on this page is a form

Dugnadsand takes no requests for help and holds no waiting list. It coordinates supply between the groups that do that work — finding out that a contractor forty miles away has material going to a skip, and getting it to a group that can use it.

The groups below decide what they can do and for whom. Contacting one of them directly is the whole of it: they know what is available this week, what their own rules are, and what happens next.

What happens to what is written here, when it is open

The need and the rough area go to the mutual aid groups covering that area, and to nobody else. Businesses and households in the same network never see it. The name and the contact details are encrypted and stay withheld until one group takes it up, at which point that group alone can read them.

What happens next is between that group and the person who asked. Nothing records what was decided, whether anybody was helped, or how it went — that belongs to them, and a field for it would be this system reaching into work it does not do.
