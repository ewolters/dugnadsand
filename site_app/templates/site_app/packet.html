What this document is not

It is not a receipt, and it is not a valuation. It records what was done and what arrived. It states no amount in money, assigns no value to any donation, and reports no total of anybody's contributed time.

It therefore cannot serve as substantiation for a tax deduction. What a gift was worth is a matter for the donor, their adviser and the relevant tax authority — a figure produced here would be an appraisal of donated property written by a platform about a donor.
