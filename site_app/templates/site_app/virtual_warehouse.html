The virtual warehouse

Material, rather than labour, is the more common constraint on a project. This describes how material held by one party is matched to a project that needs it, and the constraints the system observes while doing so.

Nothing moves until somebody needs it

Material does not enter the platform's custody. The system records a location, a description and a quantity, and evidences that material moved without stating a value.

Absence of custody means absence of title, and therefore no storage liability, no insurance obligation, and no basis on which the platform would be treated as a warehouse operator rather than a directory.

Every amount says when somebody last looked

An undated quantity is an unverified assertion about the present. Every listing therefore carries the age of its last confirmation — confirmed today, not confirmed in four weeks — under two constraints.

Only the holder may reset the timestamp. No other party is in a position to state what is present at that location. Dispatching material deliberately does not reset it: the sender knows the quantity dispatched but has not recounted the remainder, and resetting on dispatch would mark a listing as freshly verified on the basis of a removal.

Unconfirmed listings are de-emphasised rather than hidden, since the material may still be present. Only the holder can confirm this.

What the paperwork looks like

Dispatched material produces a manifest, which travels with the consignment. The recipient scans the code to confirm receipt. No account or registration is required; requiring one would mean receipts go unrecorded.

What it will not do

It will not price anything.
The value of donated material is a matter for the donor, their adviser and the relevant tax authority. A figure produced by this system would constitute an appraisal of donated property generated by a platform concerning a donor. A manifest evidences transfer; a valuation is a separate document that this system does not produce.
It will not convert material into hours.
An expression such as “two hundred board-feet equals forty hours” is an exchange rate. Hours and material are held as separate records and are never combined.
It will not classify material into types.
There are no categories and no type icons. A fixed vocabulary of materials renders two donations comparable, and comparable donations acquire a price. Descriptions are free text. Where the system matches a requirement to available stock, it matches on the unit as entered: board-feet to board-feet.
It will not present unverified stock as available.
Absence of a recent confirmation is displayed as such rather than as availability.

For a business considering listing material

The holder retains the material, the premises and the title, determines what is listed, and is the only party able to confirm what remains. The holder is notified when material is booked out of a location they hold. This is a notification rather than an approval step: listed material is already on offer, and an approval gate would condition a donation on a further permission.

The system returns evidence of transfer: a record of what was dispatched, when, to where, and confirmation of receipt. It does not return a figure for a tax return.

The underlying commitments are set out in the statement of operating policy and verified continuously at the attestation. How it works documents the remainder of the system.
